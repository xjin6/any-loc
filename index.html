功能Features

一个窗口，两个平台，全都本地One window, both platforms, all local

同一套 Python 代码同时跑在 Windows 和 macOS 上，插上什么就走哪条路径。The same Python runs on Windows and macOS, and auto-detects whatever you plug in.

点哪去哪Click to teleport

在地图上点一下，手机的定位立刻跳过去。搜索地名或直接粘贴一组经纬度也行。Click anywhere and the phone's location jumps there instantly. Or search a place name, or paste raw coordinates.

摇杆自由行走Walk with the joystick

拖摇杆或按 WASD／方向键平滑移动，四档速度（步行到驾车），按住 Shift 提速 3 倍。Drag the stick or press WASD / arrows for smooth movement. Four speed presets from walking to driving, hold Shift for 3×.

沿真实道路自动走Auto-walk real roads

依次点几个途经点，AnyLoc 用 OSRM 规划真实路线并按选定速度自动行进，可循环。Drop a few waypoints and AnyLoc routes them over real roads with OSRM, then walks the path at your chosen speed. Looping supported.

iOS 与 Android 自动识别iOS and Android, auto-detected

插安卓走 adb（免 root、免装 App、免提权）；插 iPhone 走 Apple 隧道。界面完全一样。Android goes over adb — no root, no app, no elevation. iPhone goes over Apple's tunnel. Identical UI either way.

全程本地，零上传Fully local, zero upload

没有账号、没有服务器、没有遥测。你的位置只在你的电脑和你的手机之间走。No account, no server, no telemetry. Your location never leaves the cable between your computer and your phone.

12 种语言12 languages

中文、日本語、한국어、English、Deutsch、Español、Français、Bahasa、Português、Русский、ไทย、Tiếng Việt。Chinese, Japanese, Korean, English, German, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Portuguese, Russian, Thai and Vietnamese.

怎么用How it works

插上线，五步搞定Plug in, five steps

1

打开「开发者模式」Turn on Developer Mode

设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 开发者模式 > 打开。手机会重启一次。Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode > on. The phone reboots once.

2

用数据线连接电脑Connect over USB

直连的 USB 口通常比转接器稳定。Windows 上需要装过 iTunes / Apple Mobile Device Support 才有驱动。A direct port beats a hub. On Windows you need iTunes / Apple Mobile Device Support installed for the USB driver.

3

点「信任这台电脑」Tap "Trust This Computer"

解锁手机，在弹窗里点「信任」并输入密码。Unlock the phone, tap Trust in the prompt, and enter your passcode.

4

点「连接」Hit Connect

圆点变绿就表示就绪。首次连接会下载开发者镜像，需要联网，可能要一分钟。The dot turns green when it's ready. The first connection downloads a developer image, which needs internet and can take a minute.

5

点击地图即可改变定位Click the map

保持窗口开着定位才持续生效；关窗口或点「恢复真实定位」都会还原真实 GPS。Keep the window open for the spoof to hold. Closing it — or hitting Reset GPS — restores the real location.

1

打开「开发者选项」Unlock Developer Options

设置 > 关于手机 > 连续点击「版本号」7 次。Settings > About phone > tap Build number seven times.

2

打开「USB 调试」Enable USB debugging

设置 > 系统 > 开发者选项 > USB 调试。小米／华为还需打开「USB 调试（安全设置）」。Settings > System > Developer options > USB debugging. Xiaomi and Huawei also need "USB debugging (Security settings)".

3

用数据线连接电脑Connect over USB

在手机弹窗里点「允许 USB 调试」，并勾选「一律允许」。Tap "Allow USB debugging" on the phone, and tick "Always allow".

4

点「连接」Hit Connect

需要 Android 11 或更新的系统。无需 root，无需安装任何 App，也不用管理员权限 —— adb 是随 AnyLoc 一起打包的。Needs Android 11 or newer. No root, no app to install, and no admin rights — adb ships bundled inside AnyLoc.

5

点击地图即可改变定位Click the map

同样地，关窗口或点「恢复真实定位」就会还原真实 GPS。Same as iOS: close the window or hit Reset GPS and the real location comes back.

请负责任地使用Please use this responsibly

AnyLoc 是给开发者和测试人员用的工具 —— 测试基于位置的功能、模拟出行轨迹、调试地理围栏。用它去欺骗别的服务、伪造考勤或违反某个 App 的服务条款，都是你自己的责任。有些服务会检测定位模拟并封号。AnyLoc is a tool for developers and testers — exercising location-based features, simulating trips, debugging geofences. Using it to deceive a service, fake attendance, or violate an app's terms of service is on you. Some services do detect location spoofing and will ban accounts for it.
